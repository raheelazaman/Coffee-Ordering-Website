Since 2016

Started in a garage, still stubborn about it

We began roasting on a two-kilo drum behind a hardware store, selling bags out of a folding table on Saturdays. A decade later the drum is bigger, but the approach hasn't changed: small lots, transparent sourcing, and roasting to taste rather than a schedule.

Every bean is cupped before it's approved, and every batch is logged so we can taste our way back to it.
